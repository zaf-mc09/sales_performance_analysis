# Sales Performance Analysis 📊

## Overview
This project focuses on analyzing a retail company's sales data to uncover key business insights related to revenue, profit, product performance, and regional trends.

The goal of this analysis is to simulate a real-world data analyst task: transforming raw data into actionable insights that can support business decision-making.

---

## Business Problem
The company wants to understand:
- Which regions generate the most revenue?
- Which product categories are most profitable?
- How sales change over time?
- What strategies can improve overall performance?

---

## Dataset
Source: Superstore Sales Dataset (Kaggle)  
Records: 10,000+ sales transactions  

The dataset contains information on:
- Orders and customers  
- Product categories  
- Sales and profit  
- Regional performance  
- Order dates

## Project Workflow
The analysis followed these steps:

1. Data loading and inspection  
2. Data cleaning and preprocessing  
3. Feature engineering (date extraction)  
4. Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)  
5. Data visualization  
6. Insight generation  
7. Business recommendations  

---

## Key Analysis Performed
- Total sales and profit calculation  
- Sales performance by region  
- Monthly sales trend analysis  
- Profitability by product category  

---

## Key Insights
- The West region contributes the highest total sales.
- Sales peak during Q4 (November–December)**.
- The Technology category generates the highest profit.
- Some categories show high sales but low profit margins.

---

## Business Recommendations
- Increase marketing investment in the West region.
- Prepare inventory in advance for Q4 demand spikes.
- Focus promotions on high-margin products.
- Re-evaluate pricing or costs for low-profit categories.
